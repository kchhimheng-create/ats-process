How the ATS works

Each fact is typed once; the ATS moves it everywhere else.

The ATS (applicant tracking system) is a Google Sheet with a small program built in (Apps Script). Each recruiter has their own copy. It reads the team job list, the team's Candidate Review sheet and your propose emails. This page shows what goes in, what the ATS does by itself and when, what a person still does, the safety controls, the test results, and what a team rollout needs.

What goes in

The ATS sheet

Where it shows up

You, in the sidebar You

What it is
The ATS menu opens a sidebar with five forms: Add, Log call, Match, Move and Remove. Kuru, the mascot, explains each form in a speech bubble.
What it writes
Candidates and Applications, plus one Activity Log row for every change. Typing a Stage or Contact status straight into the sheet is also logged.
When
Whenever you work: after a call, a match or a stage change.
Who
You type it. The ATS fills in IDs, dates and counts.

Gmail propose threads You write ATS reads

What it is
The emails you already send to Sales, with the subject "[Propose Candidate] One <Position> to <Company>", and the Sales replies in the same thread.
What it reads
Only threads you started, from EMAIL_SCAN_FROM onward (the last 30 days, up to 100 threads). Threads your teammates started are skipped. It never sends email.
What it writes
New candidates, new applications at Sent to Sales, and stage moves (Interview, Offer, Placed). Every finding becomes a row in the Email updates tab.
When
Every day at EMAIL_SCAN_HOUR (default 16:00), or when you click ATS → Check emails now.
Who
You write the email as usual. The ATS reads it.

Team job list ATS reads

What it is
The team's shared Google Sheet of open jobs, set by JOB_LIST_ID and JOB_LIST_TAB.
What it writes
A copy in the Jobs tab, so Match to job can offer every job by code, company or position. If a job you matched someone to disappears from the list, the application is flagged "Job no longer listed".
When
Every morning, just before the Chat digest, or when you click ATS → Refresh jobs.
Who
The ATS. It only reads the team list and never changes it.

Candidate Review sheet ATS reads

What it is
The team's monthly "Candidate Review" Google Sheet, where candidates are assigned to each recruiter. The ATS finds this month's file in Drive by its name, for example "Candidate Review November 2026".
What it reads
Every tab whose name starts with "Week". It reads the block under your name in column A (the name in REVIEW_NAME) for each day, and the candidate names in column B. File endings such as ".pdf" are removed.
What it writes
One new Candidates row per name: the name only, Source Assigned, Contact status Not contacted. Names already in Candidates, Archived candidates or deleted for good are skipped. It never changes the team sheet.
When
Every day at 9:00, again at the start of the 16:00 email scan, or when you click ATS → Check assigned candidates now. The first run adds only today's names; later runs catch up any missed days in the month.
Who
The team lists the names, as today. The ATS adds them. Leave REVIEW_NAME blank to turn it off.

Your ATS sheet ATS

What it is
One Google Sheet per recruiter, kept in their own Drive. The tabs: Candidates, Applications, Activity Log, Jobs (a mirror of the team job list), Dashboard, Settings, Email updates, Archived candidates and Archived applications.
What it keeps
One row per candidate, one row per application (candidate + job), and a history row for every change in the Activity Log. The report and Dashboard are counted from that history.
When
Always on. Three scheduled runs a day (8:00 digest, 9:00 assigned candidates, 16:00 email scan), plus whatever you click.
Who
You own it. Nothing leaves your Google account, except the Chat messages to your own Chat space.

Dashboard ATS

What it shows
Follow-ups due now, Stuck at Sales, Interviews in next 7 days, Placed this month; Pipeline now; the last 6 months; candidates by status and by source; and "Do today" lists with names.
What it reads
Candidates, Applications and the Activity Log. It holds only formulas, so nobody types into it.
When
Live. It changes the moment the sheet changes.
Who
The ATS.

Daily report Doc You click and send ATS writes

What it is
Your usual daily report Google Doc, set by REPORT_DOC_ID.
What it writes
The greeting, sections 1 to 4 and the KPI lines, built from today's Activity Log and the month so far. The whole Doc is replaced each time.
When
When you click ATS → Update daily report, usually at the end of the day. You can run it more than once; the same data gives the same report.
Who
You click, check, fill in the hand-typed parts and email it. The ATS never sends it.

Google Chat digest ATS

What it is
A short morning message in your own Chat space, sent through CHAT_WEBHOOK_URL.
What it lists
Sent to Sales applications with no update for more than STALE_DAYS days, interviews today or tomorrow, follow-ups due, and jobs no longer listed.
When
Every day at DIGEST_HOUR (default 8). If nothing needs attention, no message is sent. Two other runs post one line each when they found something: the assigned import ("👋 Added 1 assigned candidate: …") and the 16:00 email scan.
Who
The ATS. Leave the webhook blank to turn it off.

Email updates tab ATS You tick

What it is
One row per finding from the email scan, with the sentence it read as Evidence and an Open link to the email.
Statuses
"Auto-applied" (done, nothing to do), "Needs check: <reason>" (waits for your tick), "Applied" or "Ignored" (you dealt with it), "Error: <message>".
When
Filled by each scan. Needs check rows stay at the top.
Who
The ATS fills it. You tick Apply or Ignore, then click ATS → Apply ticked updates.

Inside the email scan

The scan checks three things in order: the name, the job and the stage. It acts alone only when it is sure.

Pick a sample email. These are invented, but each one shows a real rule from the code.

Why these 7 needed a check

None of the 7 was a wrong update. Each was a case where the ATS could not be sure, so it asked instead of guessing.

  • 5 had no matching job in the list. The job in the subject was not clearly in the team job list. The person picks the job code, from the drop-down when the company has jobs listed.
  • 1 had an ambiguous job. The company had several similar jobs.
  • 1 had no application. An interview reply arrived for a proposal that was itself still waiting for a job.

The 36 rows are findings from the 34 threads: a thread can give a proposal row and a stage row.

Re-running the scan never duplicates a candidate, an application or an update. Rows that were applied, ignored or ticked are kept, and their keys block repeats.

A day with the ATS

Three things run by themselves each day. Everything else happens when you click.

Scheduled runs start within the hour that is set, not at the exact minute.

8:00 · Chat digest ATS

  • Runs at DIGEST_HOUR (default 8). First it refreshes the Jobs tab from the team job list.
  • Then it posts one message to your Chat space with: "Sent to Sales, no update > 3 days" (from STALE_DAYS), "Interviews today/tomorrow", "Follow-ups due" and "Jobs no longer listed".
  • Each stale application gets a "Reminder" row in the Activity Log.
  • If nothing needs attention, no message is sent. ATS → Send digest now runs the same thing on demand.
ATS daily digest – 2026-11-19 🗓 Interviews today/tomorrow: • Dara Sok – LOT-261103-01 (Lotus Valley Co., Ltd) – 2026-11-20 📞 Follow-ups due: • Bopha Keo – due 2026-11-19

9:00 · assigned candidates ATS

  • Finds this month's Candidate Review sheet in your Drive by its name, and reads the block under your name (REVIEW_NAME) in each "Week" tab.
  • Adds each new name to Candidates: name only, Source Assigned, Contact status Not contacted. Names already in the ATS, archived or deleted are skipped.
  • The first run adds only today's names. After that it adds every day since the last run, so a missed day is caught up. Each Added row carries the day the team listed the name, so CVs Assigned counts it on that day.
  • If it added anyone, it posts one line to Chat, for example "👋 Added 2 assigned candidates: Dara Sok, Lina Chan".
  • It runs again at the start of the 16:00 scan, for names listed later in the day. ATS → Check assigned candidates now runs it at any time; it answers "No new assigned candidates." when there is nothing to add.
  • With REVIEW_NAME blank it is off, and the menu item says: "Set REVIEW_NAME in Settings to your name in the Candidate Review sheet, then try again."

During the day · sidebar actions You

  • Add candidate, Log call / note, Match to job, Move stage, Remove / restore candidate.
  • If your cursor is on a candidate or application row when you open a form, that record is already picked.
  • Every save writes the change and its Activity Log row together. The Dashboard updates at once.
  • Only one action runs at a time. If two clash, you see "ATS is busy, please try again in a moment."

16:00 · email scan ATS

  • Runs at EMAIL_SCAN_HOUR (default 16). First it runs the assigned import again, so a candidate assigned today already exists when the scan reads your proposal.
  • Then it reads your "[Propose Candidate]" threads and the Sales replies.
  • Applies what it is sure about, and lists the rest as "Needs check" at the top of the Email updates tab.
  • If it found anything, it posts one line to Chat, for example "📬 Email scan: 4 applied automatically, 1 needs a check — open Email updates."
  • ATS → Check emails now runs the same scan at any time. Then tick rows and click ATS → Apply ticked updates.

End of day · Update daily report You click ATS writes

  • ATS → Update daily report rewrites your report Doc from today's Activity Log and the month so far.
  • The finish message says what it found, for example "Daily report updated: 2 introductions, 1 proposals, 1 interviews." It also lists anything to fix, such as a missing Recycle number.
  • The whole Doc is replaced each time. Yesterday's version is in File → Version history.

Then · you email it You

  • Open the Doc and check it.
  • Type the parts the ATS leaves to you: the Recycle number, section 4 (Job Assigned) and the Recycling Sheet Link.
  • Email it as you do today. The ATS never sends the report.

How each KPI line is counted (month to date)

Working days

Counts down. It shows the Monday-to-Friday days left after today, out of the month's total, minus the days in HOLIDAYS. The first working day shows 16/17 and the last shows 0/17. In this sample, HOLIDAYS lists four days in November.

CVs Assigned

Candidates added this month with Source = Assigned. The target comes from KPI_CVS_ASSIGNED (30 in this sample; the default is 0).

Introduction

Applications moved to Sent to Sales this month, counted once per application. A proposal applied from email counts on the email's date. Target: KPI_INTRO (default 40).

Recycle

Not counted by the ATS. It is copied from the previous report. Type the day's number into the Recycle line after each update; the next update carries it forward. If it is missing, the line shows "?" and the finish message reminds you. Target: KPI_RECYCLE (default 240).

Final Interview

Applications moved to Interview this month, once per application. Target: KPI_FINAL_INTERVIEW (default 13).

Pass

Applications moved to Placed this month. Target: KPI_PASS (default 5).

Sale

The sum of Fee ($) on this month's Placed applications. A fee left blank counts as 0, so type it when you place someone. Target: KPI_SALE (default 4000).

Hit target rate

Sale divided by the Sale target, as a percentage with two decimals: 2700 ÷ 4000 = 67.50%.

Started mid-month?
Set KPI_START_MONTH (like 2026-11) and the start numbers START_CVS_ASSIGNED, START_INTRO, START_FINAL_INTERVIEW and START_SALE. They are added only in that month.
Sections 1 to 3
Today only: 1 lists applications sent to Sales today, 2 lists today's job matches, 3 lists today's HR interviews.
Section 4 and the link
Always written as shown and filled in by hand.
Deleted candidates
Still count in the KPIs, but are not listed by name.

Safety and control

The ATS is built to ask rather than guess, and to keep history rather than lose it.

Nothing is lost
Archive keeps everything, and Restore brings it back with all applications. Delete for good needs the candidate's name typed to confirm. The Activity Log keeps the history, so past reports and KPIs still add up.
One action at a time
A lock lets only one action run at once. A clash shows "ATS is busy, please try again in a moment." and nothing is half-written.
Checks first
Every check runs before anything is written. A failed save changes nothing and uses up no ID.
Offer and Placed wait for you
The email scan never moves anyone to Offer or Placed by itself. Placed closes the candidate's other applications, so it always waits for a tick.
Scan start date
The scan reads nothing before EMAIL_SCAN_FROM. If that setting is blank or not a real date, the scan stops with a message telling you to use yyyy-mm-dd or run Setup.
No duplicates
Re-scans never create the same candidate, application or update twice. The assigned import skips any name already in Candidates, in Archived candidates or deleted for good, even with different capital letters or spaces.
Old layout guard
After an update, the ATS refuses to save until Setup has run: "Your Candidates columns are out of date — click ATS → Setup first."
Your data stays yours
Everything stays in the person's own Google Sheet and Gmail. The team job list and the Candidate Review sheet are only read, never changed. The only things sent out are the Chat messages, to the person's own Chat space.

Permissions Google asks for once

Each person approves these on their own account when they first run Setup. Because the program belongs to the sheet, Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app"; the person clicks Advanced, then Go to (project name), then Allow.

  • Google Sheets: the ATS sheet itself, and reading the team job list.
  • Google Docs: rewriting the daily report Doc.
  • Gmail, read: finding and reading the "[Propose Candidate]" threads. The code only searches and reads; it never sends, labels or deletes mail.
  • Google Drive, read: finding this month's Candidate Review sheet by its name. The code only searches for it and reads it.
  • Sending to the Chat webhook: posting the digest, the assigned line and the scan line to the person's own Chat space.

Someone updating from an earlier version is asked for Drive access once, the first time the assigned import runs from the menu. They allow it, then click the same menu item again.

Rolling out to the team

Each recruiter gets their own copy. Setup takes about 15 minutes per person.

Per person

  1. Copy the templateOpen the ATS template sheet and click File → Make a copy. The program comes with the copy.
  2. Run SetupReload the copy, click ATS → Setup, and approve the permissions. Wait for "ATS is ready." If it does not appear, click Setup again. Setup creates the tabs, drop-down lists and the daily scheduled runs. It is safe to run again.
  3. Set these Settings (Value column only)
    CHAT_WEBHOOK_URL
    The address of the person's own Chat space. Blank turns the digest off.
    REPORT_DOC_ID
    Their daily report Doc. Try it on a copy of the Doc first.
    REVIEW_NAME
    Their name exactly as written in column A of the Candidate Review sheet. Capital letters and spaces around it do not matter. Blank turns the assigned import off.
    EMAIL_SCAN_FROM
    Their start day, like 2026-11-02. Setup fills in today if it is blank.
    HOLIDAYS
    This month's days off, like 2026-11-09, 2026-11-23.
    KPI_…
    Their monthly targets.
    KPI_START_MONTH, START_…
    Only if they start mid-month.
  4. TestClick Refresh jobs, Send digest now, Check emails now and Check assigned candidates now, and check each message.

What approval is needed

  • Agreement to roll the ATS out to the HR team.
  • Each person approves the Google permissions above on their own account.
  • Each person can open the team job list and the monthly Candidate Review sheet (view access is enough).
  • Agreement that the ATS writes the daily report Doc, and the person still checks and sends it.

Known limits

  • The scan reads only threads the person started, with "[Propose Candidate]" in the subject, from the last 30 days (up to 100 threads).
  • It moves stages forward only (Sent to Sales, Interview, Offer, Placed). Closed stages, such as Rejected by client, are set with Move stage.
  • Fee ($) and Recycle are typed by hand. So are section 4 and the Recycling Sheet Link.
  • A Needs check row that is not ticked is rebuilt by the next scan. Pick the job and tick Apply before then.
  • The report Doc is replaced each time; keep only the latest report in it.
  • Each copy is separate. A new version is pasted into each copy (or the template) and Setup is run again.
  • Past about 1000 rows, run Setup again to extend colours and drop-down lists.
  • The assigned import reads only this month's Candidate Review file, found by a title with "Candidate Review" and the month, for example "Candidate Review November 2026". A file named differently is not found.
  • A name spelled differently in Candidate Review and in the propose email is treated as two people. Archive the extra one.

Glossary

ATS
Applicant tracking system: where candidates, jobs and progress are kept.
Apps Script
Google's built-in way to add a small program to a Sheet. It runs on Google's side.
Sidebar
The panel on the right of the sheet that holds the forms.
Application
One candidate matched to one job. A candidate can have several.
Stage
Where an application is: Matched, Sent to Sales, Interview, Offer, Placed, or a closed stage.
Activity Log
The history tab. One row per change, with its time.
Candidate Review
The team's monthly Google Sheet that lists which candidates are assigned to each recruiter, day by day.
Propose email
The email to Sales with "[Propose Candidate]" in the subject.
Auto-applied
The scan was sure and made the change itself.
Needs check
The scan was not sure. It waits for a person to tick Apply or Ignore.
Digest
The short morning message in Google Chat.
Webhook
A private web address for a Chat space. Posting to it puts a message in that space.
Archive
Hide a candidate and their applications, with the option to restore them.
KPI
The monthly numbers in the daily report, each shown against its target.
Kuru
The ATS mascot, who explains forms and reacts to messages.
